01  /  Getting a jar in

Three steps, one screen. Then it is on the shelf and you never touch it again.

1

Frame it in the ring

A ring shows you where to put the jar mouth. That is not decoration: holding everyone to the same framing is what makes two people's colours comparable instead of two people's lighting.

2

The colour is read on your device

Pixels inside the ring get sorted by lightness and the midtone is taken. Specular highlights are thrown away, because a highlight is the colour of your kitchen light, not of your hash.

3

Fill in what you know

Strain, washer, micron, wash, cure, melt. Type what you know and skip what you do not. Nothing here is required to get a jar.

How it got here changes what it says

Captured in session
Shot live, through the site

The camera stream, in an open session, with no stored file in between. The strongest claim available, and the one worth having.

Uploaded
Chosen from your camera roll

A photo exists and it might be anyone's. Perfectly fine for logging what you have. It just does not claim more than it can.

Declared
No photo at all

Typed from memory. Still a jar, still yours, still on the shelf. An assertion, and it says so.

Every tier is open to every account, free included. Shooting a jar in your hand outranks a paying member's screenshot, and it always will. That only means something if it cannot be bought.

03  /  And none of it leaks

Show the jars. Not the receipts, the vendor, or where you were standing.

01

The photo is stripped before it leaves

GPS, camera serial and timestamps come off in your browser, before anything uploads. If the scrubber cannot verify a file is clean, it refuses to send it.

02

Encrypted with a key we never hold

Your passphrase unwraps a key that stays on your device. Notes, prices and vendors are sealed before they reach us. Lose the passphrase and the data is gone, including for us.

03

A shared shelf is a copy, not a window

Publishing sends a projection with a fixed column list. Prices, vendors, batch codes and dates cannot leak, because they were never in what left.

04

You can take it and go

Export everything whenever you want. Delete it and it is deleted. No export fee, no thirty day window, no talking to anyone first.
