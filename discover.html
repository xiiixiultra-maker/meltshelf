Shelves people chose to show.

Every shelf here was listed on purpose. Sorted by what is actually in the jars, not by who posts most. You do not need an account to look, and nothing on this page asks you to make one.

Counting listed shelves. Meltshelf has not opened to signups yet, so there is not much here on purpose. The rest of this page shows what a card carries once other shelves land, and every one of those is marked as an example so nobody has to guess which is which.

Listing is a separate switch from sharing. A shelf can be shareable by link and still never appear on this page. Nothing lands here because somebody sent a friend a link, and turning listing off removes it immediately. Prototype jars can never appear on this page: the database projection that feeds it excludes them, so there is no setting to get wrong. Wish lists are not listed here either, they stay private.

Nothing matches those filters.
